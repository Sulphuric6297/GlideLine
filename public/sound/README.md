# Sounds

- `import-trees.mp3`: "Long Woosh Glitchy Fx" by gerainsan, freesound.org/s/354176, CC0 1.0. Played as the trees come up while a site is built.
- The import's music is not in this repository. The app plays `zz-import-music.mp3` from the site's root if it is there, and nothing if it isn't. The track used on the development site is "Weightlessness" by SergeQuadrado, freesound.org/s/740609, CC BY-NC 4.0: credit is required and commercial use needs the author's commercial licence.
- The chime at the build's end is not in this repository either: the app plays `zz-import-chime.mp3` from the site's root if it is there. The file used on the development site was supplied by the owner ("Piano Chime"); its source and licence are not recorded.
- The sounds for the photo, the ground, the water, the paths and the buildings are not recordings: the app makes them as they play (`src/lib/importSound.ts`), in C major, the music's key.
